A calm estate with garden and mountain views

Ihunde Farm Lodge sits on a quiet street in Kiruhura. Each private cottage has its own bathroom, kitchen, bathrobes and free WiFi, arranged around a paved courtyard and open lawns.

Begin with a breakfast of your choice, spend the evening in the sauna, and rest knowing your car is safe on site.

Good to know

Quick answers for your stay. Need more? Call reception any time.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early arrival or late departure can be arranged on request.

Is there an airport shuttle?

Yes. Shuttle transfers are available on request. Share your flight details when you book.

Is parking available?

Free, secure parking is available on site for all guests.

Do you host weddings and conferences?

Yes. We have party gardens and a conference room. Contact us for a quote.

Are children welcome?

Children are welcome, and family rooms are available.
